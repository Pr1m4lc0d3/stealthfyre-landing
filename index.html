One system.
Every stove you need.

StealthFyre is a single set of interlocking 24-gauge stainless steel panels. The same panels assemble into a rocket stove, a tunnel stove, a windbreak, a chimney — then break down flat and go back in the pack.

No welds. No tools. No single-purpose gear taking up space you don't have.

  • Interlocking panels — assembled by hand, in the field
  • Packs flat, carries light
  • Stainless steel, built to keep going
  • Reconfigures instead of being replaced
